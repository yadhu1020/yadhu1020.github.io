Ongoing · Master thesis · Ghent University

PZT electro-optically tunable Vernier cavities

Electrical and optical characterization of fabricated PZT Vernier cavities.

Details +
  • Two coupled microring resonators with slightly different free spectral ranges, placed between an input and a drop waveguide.
  • PZT gives electro-optic tuning: a small shift of one resonator moves the selected wavelength by a full free spectral range.
  • Current work: electrical and optical characterization of the fabricated devices.
  • Status: ongoing.
Ongoing · Ghent University

High-efficiency apodized grating couplers

Apodized fibre-to-chip grating couplers designed for high coupling efficiency.

Details +
  • Fibre-to-chip grating couplers whose period and fill factor change along the grating.
  • The apodization shapes the emitted beam to match the fibre mode, which raises coupling efficiency.
  • Simulated in Ansys Lumerical FDTD.
  • Status: ongoing.
2025 – 2026 · IIA Bangalore

Liquid crystal variable retarders for a spectropolarimeter

Master's thesis. A Berreman 4×4 simulator, a full-Stokes polarimeter and first fabrication steps.

Details +
  • Developed liquid crystal variable retarders for a multi-line spectropolarimeter at IIA, for solar magnetic field observations.
  • Wrote a Python Berreman 4×4 simulator and used simulated annealing to find passive quarter-wave retarder geometries for nematic cells. Validated it against published cholesteric reflection spectra.
  • Designed, calibrated and validated a full-Stokes polarimeter with pixel-wise Mueller matrix retrieval.
  • Fabrication training at the CREST campus: substrate preparation, spin coating and alignment layers.

Sharp bends in photonic crystal waveguides

High-transmission 120° bends for compact on-chip routers and splitters.

Details +
  • Simulation-driven design methods for high-transmission 120° bends in photonic crystal waveguides.
  • Simulated with COMSOL, Lumerical FDTD and MIT Photonic Bands.
  • Aimed at ultra-compact routers and Y-splitters.
  • Summer research internship.
2024 · TIFR Hyderabad

Tunable topological photonic metasurface

Triangular-hole Sb₂S₃ metasurface: a symmetry-broken BIC for tunable circularly polarized light.

Details +
  • Triangular holes break the C2 symmetry, so the bound state in the continuum (BIC) near the Γ point leaks into a radiative mode.
  • This symmetry engineering generates circularly polarized light at the nanoscale.
  • Studied how the band structure of Sb₂S₃ changes across its phase transition, and how that changes the emitted light.
  • Theoretical study in COMSOL and Lumerical, with cleanroom exposure. VSRP summer internship. Led to a poster at OPTIQ 2025.
2024 · ISP, CUSAT

Dirac modes in photonic crystal fibres

Bachelor's thesis. Dirac-point guiding in photonic crystal fibres, simulated with FETD.

Details +
  • Studied Dirac modes in photonic crystal fibres with the finite-element time-domain (FETD) method.
  • In one-dimensional photonic crystals, a Dirac point (a conical singularity in the band structure) suppresses radiation loss and gives a new guided mode.
  • A step beyond conventional fibre guiding, with applications in sensing and nonlinear optics.
  • Bachelor's thesis. Presented as a poster at OPTIQ 2025.
2023 · IIST Trivandrum

Two-colour ratio pyrometry

Two-colour imaging pyrometer, calibrated and used to map the temperature of a lamp filament.

Details +
  • Non-contact temperature measurement of hot objects that emit like a black body, based on Planck radiation.
  • An image doubler with green and blue bandpass filters on its two arms images the target on a CCD at two wavelengths. The ratio of the two signals gives the surface temperature.
  • Calibrated with a standard intensity source and a fibre-coupled spectrometer, then used to map the temperature across the filament of a 60 W incandescent lamp.
  • Summer research internship.
